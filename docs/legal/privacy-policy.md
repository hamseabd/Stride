## 1. Who We Are

Stride is a personal productivity coaching service that helps you set goals,
track daily progress, and reflect on your work each week.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at: strideproductivity@gmail.com

---

## 2. What Information We Collect

### Information you provide directly
- **Phone number** — collected during signup to send you SMS check-ins and
  coaching messages.
- **Name or username** — used to personalise your experience.
- **Task and goal data** — the projects, tasks, and goals you create inside
  Stride, including estimates, statuses, and progress notes.
- **Check-in responses** — your daily answers to what you did, what you are
  doing, and what is blocking you.

### Information collected automatically
- **Message timestamps** — when messages are sent and received.
- **Usage patterns** — aggregated data about how you use Stride (e.g. which
  days you check in, how often tasks are completed on time). This is used
  only to improve your coaching experience.

### Information we do NOT collect
- We do not collect payment information directly (handled by Stripe or
  similar if billing is added).
- We do not collect location data.
- We do not collect device identifiers or browser cookies.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Send you SMS check-in prompts and coaching summaries you have opted in to
  receive.
- Generate personalised productivity insights based on your historical data.
- Improve the Stride coaching model over time.
- Respond to your support requests.

We do **not** use your information to:

- Send unsolicited marketing messages.
- Profile you for advertising purposes.
- Sell or rent your data to any third party.

---

## 4. SMS Messaging

### Consent
You consent to receive SMS messages from Stride when you sign up and reply
YES to our confirmation message. Consent is voluntary. You will not receive
SMS messages from Stride until you have confirmed opt-in.

### Message frequency
Message frequency varies, typically 1-3 messages per day including daily
check-in prompts, weekly planning messages, and weekly review summaries.

### Message and data rates
Standard message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply.

### Opting out
Reply **STOP** to any Stride message at any time to unsubscribe. You will
receive one final confirmation and no further messages will be sent. You can
re-enrol at any time by contacting us.

### Help
Reply **HELP** to any Stride message or email strideproductivity@gmail.com for assistance.

## 5. How We Store Your Data

Your data is stored securely on Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure
located in the United States (us-east-1 region). We use AWS DynamoDB for
data storage. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you request
account deletion, we will delete your data within 30 days, except where
retention is required by law.

## 7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

- **Service providers** — as listed in Section 6, solely to operate Stride.
- **Legal requirements** — if required by law, court order, or government
  authority.
- **Business transfer** — if Stride is acquired or merged, your data may
  transfer to the new owner under the same terms as this policy.

You have the right to:
