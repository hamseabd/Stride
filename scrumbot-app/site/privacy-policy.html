Stride / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated March 9, 2026

Stride is an AI-powered productivity coaching service delivered via SMS. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

What we collect

  • Phone number — to send and receive SMS messages.
  • Message content — your tasks, goals, check-in responses, and conversations with Stride.
  • Usage patterns — task completion rates, planning habits, and response timing. This powers Stride's pattern analysis.
  • Consent records — timestamps and status of your opt-in and opt-out actions.

How we use it

  • Coaching — to run your planning, check-in, and review sessions.
  • Pattern analysis — to identify habits and provide personalised recommendations.
  • Service improvement — to make Stride's coaching better over time.
  • Compliance — to maintain consent records and meet legal requirements.

Data sharing

Mobile opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We do not sell your data. We share it only with providers that help us run Stride:

  • Twilio — SMS delivery. Processes your phone number and message content. Privacy policy.
  • Amazon Web Services — cloud infrastructure. Data stored in US data centres. Privacy policy.
  • Anthropic — AI processing. Message content is sent to Claude to generate coaching responses. Privacy policy.

These providers are contractually obligated to use your data only to provide services to us.

Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. If you reply STOP, we stop all messaging and retain only consent records for compliance. You can request full deletion at any time.

Text messaging

When you opt in to Stride, you consent to receive recurring automated SMS messages related to productivity coaching. Message frequency varies, typically 1-3 messages per day. Standard message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply.

  • Reply STOP to unsubscribe from all messages at any time.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or email strideproductivity@gmail.com.

Your rights

  • Opt out — reply STOP to any message to stop all communication.
  • Stop reminders — reply NO REMINDERS to stop proactive messages only.
  • Resume reminders — reply REMIND ME to restart proactive messages.
  • Get help — reply HELP for assistance.
  • Delete your data — email strideproductivity@gmail.com.
  • Access your data — email us to request a copy.

Security

Your data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AWS encryption). Access to production systems is restricted and logged.

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you via SMS before they take effect.
